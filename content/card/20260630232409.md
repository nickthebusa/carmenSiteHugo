---
title: history of rifing
---
Rifing is a technology that takes its name from Dr. Royal Rife - a scientist, researcher and inventor who managed to discover an unfailing method to weaken and destroy pathogens (viruses, bacteria, parasites and more). He discovered that if he broadcasted a specific frequency, one type of organism would explode or implode. He further demonstrated that ability to kill microbes by broadcasting the exact match of the frequency to the microbe. He was one of the first in the world to use these electromagnetic frequencies for therapeutic use and for the treatment of infectious disease caused by pathogens. Many years after his successes, the technology invented by him has evolved. The modern rife equipment can not only apply the frequencies for disabling individual microorganisms (killing frequencies) but also a number of frequencies to provide healing.
